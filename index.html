Jump In Shorts Uploader

Jump In Shorts Uploader is a personal tool that uploads short English-lesson videos to the owner's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API. It is not a public service and it has no other users.

Each video is a one-minute workplace-English lesson produced by the owner. The tool uploads the video file, sets its title, description, and audience setting, and publishes it to the channel the owner picks during sign-in.

Terms of use: this tool is for the owner's personal use only. It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channels and uploads only videos the owner produced. It does not collect, sell, or share personal data with anyone.
