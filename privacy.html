Privacy Policy — Jump In Shorts Uploader

Effective date: November 6, 2026.

What this app does

Jump In Shorts Uploader is a personal tool for one person. It uploads short English-lesson videos to that person's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

What data it accesses

When the owner signs in, the app receives permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channels (the youtube.upload scope) and to read basic information about those channels and videos, such as titles and view counts. It does not request access to anything else.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to check the status of those uploads. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No analytics are collected.

Where the data is stored

Sign-in credentials stay on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google Cloud project. Video files are the owner's own productions.
